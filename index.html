Chapter IV

Monsters and Bosses

Grouped by the floor they haunt. Drop chances are per kill; a boss's loot goes into its chest.

Chapter V

Adventurers of Ashvale

Five travellers around Ashvale each carry a six-step story. Talk to them once to begin; every step pays the moment it's done, and each story ends with a Boss Key.

Chapter VI

Boss Chests and Boss Keys

When a bottom-floor boss falls it leaves a single chest. Everyone who dealt at least 5% of its health can open it with one Boss Key. Inside: a random spread of cash, materials and gems, a 5% chance of a bonus Boss Key, and a 4% chance of an anime-style cosmetic. Boss Keys come from bosses, adventurer quests, achievements and daily rewards.

Chapter VII

Game Passes

One-time purchases that follow you to every island.
